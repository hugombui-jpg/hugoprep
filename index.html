FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Common questions from parents and students. Still curious? Just reach out.

What subjects do you tutor?
We cover all core subjects for grades 1–8: math, science, English, and French. On top of that, we offer specialized prep for the UTS entrance exam, gifted program testing, and science fair projects. Hugo covers every subject; Ryan focuses on math and science.
Who are the tutors?
Hugo Bui and Ryan Zander, two Grade 9 students at UTS (one of Canada's top schools) and gold medalists at the 2026 Toronto Science Fair. Because we're current students, we teach the exact curriculum and admissions processes your child is facing right now.
How much does tutoring cost?
A flat $40 per hour for everything: regular tutoring, UTS prep, gifted prep, and science fair coaching all cost the same. Your first 1-hour session is free, the intro consultation is free, and there are no contracts or hidden fees.
Can we do online sessions?
Absolutely. Ryan teaches online only, and Hugo is available either in-person across Toronto or online, whichever works best for your family's schedule and location.
How do we get started?
Book a free intro on our scheduling page, or send a message using the form below. We'll set up a quick intro chat, learn about your child's goals, match them with the right tutor, and book the first session.
What makes you different from other tutors?
We're top students teaching the exact material we're learning right now, so our knowledge is current and our advice is real. Being close in age also means kids feel comfortable asking questions, and our gold medals and UTS spots prove the approach works.
How long are sessions?
Sessions are typically one hour, which works well for most students. We can adjust the length based on age, attention span, and goals, so just let us know what works best for your family.
